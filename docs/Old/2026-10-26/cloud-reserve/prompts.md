## C — Скобы / BRACKETS

## D — Лепестки / PETALS

Flat tapered pentagonal armored tile drone with broader front and clipped narrow rear, graphite underside and one recessed belly emitter, two short lateral propulsion slots. A compact robotic petal as solid engineered flying plate, no organic textures.

## F — Клинья / KEELS
