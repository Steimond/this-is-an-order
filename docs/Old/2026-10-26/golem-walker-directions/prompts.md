Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE professional RTS robot concept sheet for the GOLEM, a heavy quadruped melee protector. Reference image is MATERIAL AND FACTION STYLE ONLY: pale muted grey metal armor, graphite joints, very restrained cyan sensor accents, brutal readable high-tech machine with broad panels and bevels, minimal small surface details. DO NOT copy reference wheels or antenna. Four legs exactly, no hands, no guns, no barrels, no rockets, no energy shields, no extra appendages, no humanoid head. Robot built from nanites but completely solid. All armor is physical. Composition: neutral warm-grey studio sheet, large main three-quarter front view in neutral bilateral symmetric standing pose with all FOUR feet grounded, a smaller same-design deployed view below right showing body lowered, TWO physical shield leaves extending outwards and forward to shelter allies BEHIND, never circular shield. Keep both views same design. All parts fully within frame. Main view 75% of sheet, readable silhouette at RTS scale, modest wear no greeble. No action effects, no scenery. Label only variant letter and name. Deployment is mechanical hinge movement, not magical morphing.

## A — RAM

Low broad wedge body, forward sloping monolithic prow, four short stout articulated legs, front pair broad striking ram feet. Two huge flat flank armor plates hinged near front shoulders unfold outward and forward into a shallow concave frontal barricade. Very low brutal silhouette.

## B — BASTION

Tall compact square body above four upright stout pillar legs, front feet broad industrial impact shoes, no hands. Two tall rectangular armor doors lie close along sides then swing forward and outward to form a tall straight-sided frontal barricade. Upright monumental silhouette, not humanoid.

## C — CARAPACE

Broad flattened hexagonal carapace hull, four low outward angled legs with large front impact wedges. Shell consists of one central spine and TWO large outer armored shell leaves, each rotates down outward and forward into a low wide frontal screen. Beetle-like engineered robot, no biological texture, no extra legs.

## D — ANVIL

Compact short rectangular central core, two oversized angular front hammer-foot legs and two smaller but sturdy rear support legs. Distinct powerful front-heavy silhouette. Two large thick shoulder armor slabs fold outward and forward into a frontal protective wall. No separate arms or guns, front legs ARE impact weapons.

## E — GATE

Long narrow central chassis with four medium-height broad legs, negative space under belly; two very tall narrow shield leaves stowed vertically close along hull sides like armored book covers. They pivot forward and outward as a broad two-leaf frontal gate, body lowers. Clean cathedral-like industrial silhouette without decoration.

## F — PLINTH

Compact broad octagonal low armored central body with chamfered planar surfaces and four evenly spaced heavy bent legs, front feet blunt chisels. Two wraparound gently curved thick side armor shells pivot forward and outward to form a shallow curved physical breastwork. Strong continuous curved plate surfaces combined with angular feet, functional hightech not sleek toy.
