## Юниты

Use case: stylized-concept. Completely new art direction for an original high-tech RTS robot faction, designed for strong readability at small top-down gameplay scale. Premium elegant advanced autonomous robotics, smooth aerodynamic continuous shells and precise intentional negative spaces. Solid manufactured robots assembled by nanites; NOT liquid chrome creatures. Design language: satin cool pearl-gray ceramic shells, dark blue-graphite recessed joints and undercuts, one narrow pale cyan functional sensor per object, very sparse fine seams. Around 80% broad calm unbroken surface, 15% dark structural separation, 5% luminous detail. Large softly chamfered curves, asymmetric details only where functional. Each design uses only 2-4 primary masses; very few secondary pieces. Hidden mechanisms. No exposed cables, pipes, pistons, bolts, greebles, gratings, scaffold clutter, rust, yellow stripes, spikes, jewel cores, petal filigree, excessive glowing lines. Manufactured high-tech, not primitive, not cute household appliances. Nanite fluid is only a small black assembly puddle in a dedicated process inset. Soft neutral studio lighting, cool light gray background, professional restrained industrial concept design painting, clean gallery grid, each object fully visible at consistent isometric angle. Include a small BLACK SILHOUETTE next to each object, no ornament. Keep real usable legs and clear support structure, no magic levitation for ground units. Clear concise Russian name labels only. Original designs, not copies of another game.
